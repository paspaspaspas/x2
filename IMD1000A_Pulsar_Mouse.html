Pulsar X2 "CrazyLight"

The X2 CrazyLight is a 35g ultra-lightweight gaming mouse designed for speed, precision, and competitive esports performance. Its lightweight design reduces wrist fatigue, while the 8K polling rate delivers fast, low-latency response for smoother gameplay. Powered by the XS-1 flagship sensor, it supports up to 32,000 DPI, 750 IPS tracking speed, and 50g acceleration for accurate and consistent tracking. Durable optical switches rated for 100 million clicks ensure reliable long-term performance without double-click issues. Recommended for gamers who want an ultra-light gaming mouse with fast response, precise control, and high-end performance. If you're looking for a competitive FPS mouse with low latency and accuracy, the X2 CrazyLight is a strong choice.
